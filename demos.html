Demo 2 — before and after: the local listing

This is an illustration. It is not one business's data — it is the pattern we kept finding when we looked at trades and small shops across South East London in 2026. The "before" column is what that pattern looks like; the "after" is the same business with the free, basic things put right.

Before

  • Google listing not claimed — anyone, including a competitor, can suggest edits.
  • No opening hours, so people assume you are shut and ring someone else.
  • Wrong category, so you don't show when someone searches for the job you actually do.
  • No phone number on the listing — one tap fewer, and you lose the call.
  • A website link that either doesn't exist or lands on a page that is broken on a phone.
  • No photos, no reviews answered, no idea what you charge or where you work.

After

  • Listing claimed and verified, so the details on it are yours.
  • Correct hours, correct services, correct area — including what you don't cover.
  • Local phone number on the listing, so the call comes straight to you.
  • One clear page: what you do, where you work, how to reach you, how you charge.
  • Photos that load fast on a phone, because that is where the customer is.
  • Reviews answered honestly — including the bad ones, which we never hide or invent.
What we will not do: invent a review, invent a statistic, invent a case study, or put a countdown timer on this page. We also won't name another local business to make them look bad — when we show you a real before-and-after, it will be with the permission of the business it belongs to. Until then, this stays an illustration and says so.
